###### Help Desk Investigation Process



1\. Understand the symptoms

What exactly isn't working? 

What is working? 

When did the problem start?



2\. Identify possible causes

What are the most likely explanations? 

What evidence supports each possibility?



3\. Investigate systematically   

What checks would help eliminate potential causes? 

What would you check first?



4\. Reach a conclusion

What do you believe is happening, and how confident are you? 

What evidence is still missing?



5\. Resolve or escalate

What can you reasonably do yourself? 

When do you need administrator assistance?



6\. Document the outcome

Record the symptoms, investigation, findings, actions and any remaining issues.
